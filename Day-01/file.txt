Tokens and statements are the building blocks of your code, where tokens are the smallest individual units (like keywords or symbols) 
and statements are the complete instructions that perform an action.

Building on our previous talk about how Python works, here is the breakdown of these fundamental building blocks:

●
Tokens & Identifiers: Tokens are the tiny "words" of Python. An Identifier is a specific type of token you create to name things, like a variable or a function.
●
Comments: These are notes for humans that Python ignores. Use # for single lines and triple quotes """ """ for multi-line descriptions.
●
Variables: These are containers for storing data. You must follow Naming Rules: they can't start with a number, can't use special symbols (except _), and are case-sensitive.
